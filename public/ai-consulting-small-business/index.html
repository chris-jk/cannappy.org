Questions small business owners ask about AI consulting

What is AI consulting for a small business?

AI consulting for a small business means finding the routine tasks software can now do, such as answering common questions or drafting replies, and setting that up with the tools the business already uses. Cannappy does it as a fixed-price project starting at $2,500.

How much does AI consulting cost?

Cannappy charges a fixed $2,500 for a first AI project that automates one task, $2,500 to $10,000 for larger projects, and $150 an hour for advice.

Do I need technical staff to use AI in my business?

No. Cannappy sets it up, tests it and shows your team how to use it. You need someone who knows the task, not someone who knows AI.

Will AI replace my employees?

That is not the aim. Cannappy's projects take the repetitive part of a job, such as first drafts and common questions, and leave decisions and customers to people.

How long does a first AI project take?

A first AI project with Cannappy usually takes two to three weeks from the first call to a working setup.
