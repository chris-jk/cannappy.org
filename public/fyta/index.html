The honest part

Yes, fyta has ads. Just not the ones you hate.

Nobody actually minds an ad they can scroll past. What people can't stand are the ones that interrupt the video, force sound, or block you before you can even start. fyta removes exactly those — and keeps only the quiet kind.

Gone The ads that interrupt you

  • Pre-roll before the video plays
  • Mid-roll that cuts your video in half
  • The second ad YouTube stacks when you look away
  • Unskippable pods with forced sound
  • Pop-ups gating you before you can use the app

Kept The kind nobody minds

  • An occasional card in the feed you scroll right past
  • Never in the player, never on top of a video
  • Never before you can hit play
  • Never with forced audio
  • That's how a free app stays free — without becoming the thing you were escaping

The trick fyta kills: if you don't tap "skip," YouTube plays you a second ad — betting you glanced away. fyta just plays the video. No skipping, no waiting, no punishment for not paying attention to an ad.

How to install (Chrome, Edge, Brave)

Download the file above and unzip it.

Open chrome://extensions — copy and paste it into your address bar, since browsers don’t allow direct links to that page.

Turn on Developer mode (top-right toggle).

Click Load unpacked and select the fyta-extension folder.

Open any YouTube video — a “Watch ad-free on fyta” button appears.

Android

Download the APK from the latest release, then open it.

If Android asks, allow installs from this source — it's a one-time toggle.

Open fyta and start watching. Optionally import your subscriptions from the "You" tab.

iPhone & iPad

iOS doesn't allow this kind of app in the App Store, so fyta installs by sideloading (e.g. AltStore / SideStore).

Follow the iOS install guide on the releases page. Heads-up: on iOS, playback tops out around 360p — an Apple-side limitation of how the streams decode.

Why isn't it on the Play Store or App Store? Because it's the honest version of what you want — and the stores don't allow apps that strip YouTube's ads. Downloading it directly is the trade-off for an app that actually does the job and doesn't answer to a store that can delete it.

Questions

The stuff people ask.

Is it really free?

Yes. No subscription, no paywall, no "premium to remove ads." The only ads are the occasional card in the feed you scroll past — never in the player.

Do I need a Google account?

No. fyta works fully anonymously — search, play, history, likes, and follows all work without signing into anything. If you want your real subscriptions, you import them from a Google Takeout file; you still never sign in.

What data does fyta collect?

The app itself collects nothing — your history, likes, and follows stay on your device. The one exception is the feed ads, served by Google AdMob, which use an advertising identifier like ads in most free apps do.

Will it keep working when YouTube changes things?

fyta plays the actual video stream rather than fighting YouTube's ad system, so it doesn't break the way "auto-skip" apps do. When YouTube shifts something under the hood, the app updates — grab the latest release.

Is this safe to install?

The APK is the same app, just delivered directly instead of through a store. Android will ask you to allow installing it — that's the standard prompt for any app from outside the Play Store, not a warning that something's wrong.
